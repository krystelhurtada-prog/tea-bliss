Our Background

The History of Milk Tea

Milk tea is a popular beverage enjoyed by people around the world. It combines tea with milk and other ingredients to create a smooth, creamy, and refreshing drink.

Over the years, milk tea has become especially popular among young people because of its variety of flavors, toppings, and customizable sweetness. Different countries have created their own styles and versions of milk tea.

About Tea Bliss
